We are Lyra.

We are a team of designers dedicated to creating stunning and effective editorial designs.

At Lyra & Co., we transform raw data and information into meaningful and memorable visual stories. As your editorial design partner, we understand that every publication has a unique identity. That’s why we provide a creative touch that aligns with your communication goals. Backed by experienced graphic designers, we handle the entire layout process, from typography selection and visual hierarchy to final production, so you can focus on the quality of your content.
